The winter list, Nº 14

Five books we can't stop lending out. Tap to add them to your shelf.

1

The Orchard Ledger

Sam Okafor

Three generations keep the books of a fading apple farm. Nobody is doing the math right, including the narrator.

2

Salt & Circuitry

Priya Nand

Seventeen poems about repair: boards, bodies, and everything that carries a current you can't see.

3

Field Guide to Grief

R. Tate

Essays written the year everything moved. Practical, unsentimental, and a little bit funny — which surprised us most.

4

Night Water

Odile Marchant

A lighthouse keeper, a drowned map, and the year the tide stopped asking permission. The best translation of the year.

5

The Quiet Cartographer

trans. Odile Marchant

A quiet novel from the Baltic: a mapmaker who erases her own village from every chart. Untranslatable, and translated anyway.
