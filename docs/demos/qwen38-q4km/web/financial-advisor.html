What we do

Four disciplines, one standard of care.

We practice like attorneys and think like librarians: everything filed, everything explained, nothing hidden behind a platform.

01 Wealth Planning A living document of your money: goals, obligations, values, and the sequence of decisions that connect them. Revised annually, never shelf-ware. The foundation

The Client Journey

How a family works with us.

No onboarding portal, no scripted sales call. A slow, deliberate beginning — because trust must be earned in the first ninety days.

i.

Listen

The first two hours are yours to fill. We ask about the business, the children, the fears. There is no pitch — and often, no follow-up needed.

Two hours · No fee
ii.

Map

We build the full picture: assets, obligations, insurance, tax posture, and the family's stated values — filed where you can always see it.

4–6 weeks
iii.

Architect

A written plan you can hold up: allocations, income strategy, estate mechanics, and the costs of every recommendation, in plain language.

Your document
iv.

Steward

Quarterly reviews, an annual letter, and a phone number that goes to a person. We measure our success decades later, not next month.

Ongoing · For the long view
