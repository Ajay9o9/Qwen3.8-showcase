House Amenities

Every small thing, quietly cared for

We keep the house unhurried. No concierge desk, no queue — just a team that learns your name by the second morning.

Sea-Facing Infinity Pool

Heated to 27°, open at dawn. The waterline disappears into the horizon — or you do, briefly.

The Fig & Vine Kitchen

A daily-changing five-course menu built on the day's boats, our orchard and the market at Vathi village.

Stone Bathhouse & Spa

Two treatment rooms, a cedar hammam and cold seawater showers. Book the sunset ritual — it sells out.

Private Chef & Cellar

Table on the rocks, cypress shade, and a cellar of 300 bottles of island and coastal Greece.

Boat & Kayak Hire

A classic wooden ketch, glass-bottom kayaks and paddleboards, launched from our private cove.

Warm Welcome, Warm Staff

Seven people, all of them here on purpose. They will remember how you take your coffee.

Beyond the House

Local experiences, arranged with care

We know this coast the way it should be known — slowly. Ask your host; we will arrange it all, quietly.

01

Dawn Sailing on the Miren

Three hours aboard our 1968 wooden ketch, past the monk-seal cove and a swim at the hidden islet. Coffee on board, always.

3 hours · from the pier
02

The Fisherman's Table

Board the day-boat with Nikos, cook over the coals what he catches, and eat it where he landed it. No menu. No hurry.

Half day · seasonal
03

Olive Grove & Mill Visit

A morning with the Papadaki family pressing first-crop oil, followed by lunch in the shade of two hundred-year-old trees.

4 hours · October–November
04

Stargazing in the Cave

After dark, we walk to the sea cave where no light reaches. Our astronomer-guide charts the constellations overhead.

2 hours · new moon nights
05

Celadon Clay Workshop

Throw your own cup at the ceramics house in Vathi. It's fired and sent home to you in the winter — a little piece of the coast.

3 hours · by appointment
06

The Long Lunch

Nothing is planned, which is the plan. A basket, a blanket, and an hour of the most unbothered swimming you'll find.

Unscheduled · on request
