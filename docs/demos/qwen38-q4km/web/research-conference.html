§ 02 — Call for Papers

Submit your working truth

Double-blind, rolling review, decisions in 21 days. Abstracts open now; full papers due 30 April.

We accept full papers (8–12 pages), short notes (3 pages), and preliminary reports for the poster halls. Every accepted submission must ship with reproducible code or a public data plan — this is not a suggestion, it is the house style.

Papers are reviewed by a rotated pool of 482 registered reviewers, with one guaranteed reviewer outside your subfield. Rejections include structured feedback, and withdrawn papers may resubmit once with a rebuttal.

§ 05 — Venue

The Kamo Riverside Institute

A 1920s research station rebuilt for 2026. Seven minutes from Kyoto Station, one walk away from the river.

Concrete, timber, and a rooftop that holds 300 people at a time.

The Institute keeps its original lecture hall, its new lab floors, and an unusually long garden. All sessions are captioned live, and the concourse café serves the conference’s own (surprisingly good) pour-over.

  • Kyoto Station → venue 7 min · Bus 26
  • KIX airport → venue 85 min · Haruka + Bus 26
  • Delegate rooms 410 · book before 30 Jun
  • Accessibility Step-free, captioned, quiet room
