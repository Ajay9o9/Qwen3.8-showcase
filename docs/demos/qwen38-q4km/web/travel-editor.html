Destination 069 — Vesteraalen, Norway

The light refuses to set

For six weeks each winter, the Lofoten archipelago lives inside a slow green dusk. We spent a month with reindeer herders, fish-cure crews and stargazers on the ridge — and learned that the darkest hour up here is the most luminous of any we've recorded.
