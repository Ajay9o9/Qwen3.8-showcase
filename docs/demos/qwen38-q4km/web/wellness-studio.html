The ritual

Every class begins the same way, and that is the point.

Ritual is how the nervous system learns it is safe to soften. At STILL / FORM, each session opens with a shared silence, a settling of breath, and a clear intention — so you can arrive exactly as you are and leave somewhere steadier.

01

Arrive & settle

Five unhurried minutes of seated breath. No phones, no rush, no catching up. Just the room.

02

Move with intention

Slow, deliberate sequences that meet your body where it is — never more, never less.

03

Close in stillness

Every class ends in savasana, every single time. The rest is part of the practice.
