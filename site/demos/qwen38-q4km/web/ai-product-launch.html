Capabilities

One model, the whole stack.

Every surface of a production model is engineered, not bolted on. Here's what ships in the box.

Native reasoning

Adaptive chain-of-thought with a budget dial. ORBIT-1 spends tokens on thinking only when the problem is worth it — and tells you how many.

think_budget: off → 64k · streaming thoughts

Vision & multimodal

Images, diagrams, video frames and UI screenshots in a single native stream. Grounding, OCR and chart synthesis without a second model.

up to 4K tiles · 128 frames / video

Deterministic tool use

Typed function schemas with structured outputs, parallel tool calls, and a hard contract: if the call is invalid, the model retries — it never hallucinates a signature.

strict mode · 0% schema drift in 1B calls

2M-token context

Feed it a codebase, a contract, a year of logs. Attention is sparse-scheduled so cost and latency scale sub-linearly with length.

TTFT stays < 40 ms at 1M tokens

Full and parameter-efficient fine-tunes on your data in under an hour. Deploy to a private shard, version them, roll back, A/B against the base model.

private shards · 1 hr median train time

Safety as infrastructure

Red-team rubrics run on every training run, a separate guardrail endpoint for live inference, and per-project policy profiles your compliance team actually reads.
