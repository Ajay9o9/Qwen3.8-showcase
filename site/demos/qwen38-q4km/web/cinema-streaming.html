The Last
Lighthouse

"A masterpiece of restraint — the sea, the storm, and the silence between them."

After forty years keeping the light at Cape Verdon, keeper Elin Marlowe must decide whether to let the lighthouse go dark — or relight the fires she has spent a lifetime burying. Shot in the off-season on the Faroe Islands, it is a film that holds its breath for two hours and then releases it all at once.
