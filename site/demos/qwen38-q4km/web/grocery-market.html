The GOODROOT promise

Small enough to name every farmer, careful enough to name every field.

Harvested at dawn

Cut before 7am, cold-chained the whole way. Nothing sits in a warehouse overnight — the daygap from soil to shelf is under 12 hours.

Fixed 2-hour slot

Delivered Tuesday and Thursday, before noon in your chosen 2-hour window. Live driver tracking, text on the last turn.

90-mile radius

Every farm on our map is within 90 miles of your postcode. Your basket card lists the name of each field it came from.

No-waste crating
