From the shoreline

Three places where the water
started to answer back.

No two coastlines are the same. Each project is designed with — never for — the people who live beside it.

Active · Year 2 of 3

Mangrove Return, Palenque Estuary

Two years ago this estuary was a bare mud flat. This month we counted 9,200 surviving seedlings and the first fiddler crabs of the decade. The fishing co-op plants two rows for every net they haul.

Growing · Seed stage

Kelp Cathedral, Kelp Cove

Twelve thousand kelp holdfasts, set by hand from kayaks in the dark. Sonar now shows canopy growth at 30% of the healthy baseline — and the otters have moved back to the cove's north shelf.

Opening · Spring 2026

Dune Guardians, Casca Bay

The bay's last high dunes were vanishing at a meter a year. New native grasses and buried breakwaters have cut erosion to under 10 cm a season. A lighthouse-keeper's granddaughter now runs the watch log.

From the field journal

Notes written with salt on the page.

Unfiltered dispatches from our field leads. This is what recovery actually sounds like.

Mar 14 · Palenque Verified

First heron pair on the mudflat in eleven years. She stood on the exact stake we planted in row 14 and didn't move for an hour. The crabs came back last month, the fish are following. The estuary is voting with its feet.

Marina R.Field Lead · Estuary Program
Mar 9 · Kelp Cove Verified

Survey dive at 06:40, kelp still black and cold. Canopy density 30% of baseline — up from 8% in autumn. We found an otter print in the sand at the north shelf on the way out. Noted, sketched, and not told to anyone for another day.

Dev O.Dive Technician · Kelp Program
Mar 3 · Casca Bay Watch Log

Storm passed at 3 AM, 40 knots. The new dune grasses bent and held. Erosion gauge reads +4 cm gain instead of the usual half-meter loss. Old Amélia said nothing, which means everything. I'm going to write that down too.

Tomas C.Dune Steward · Casca Bay
