The Kitchen

Elena Marchetti

Chef & Founder

Raised between a Ligurian kitchen and a Provençal market, Elena built Casa Oro around a single idea — that the Mediterranean's finest moments are its golden hours, and that a restaurant should hold them still. Every dish begins at the hearth: a wood-burning fire that anchors the room — and follows the day-boat catch, the orchard, and the herb farm on the hillside behind the house.

Her menu changes with the tide and the harvest, never with the calendar. It has held two Michelin stars since 2019.

"I don't cook the coast. I turn on the light and let the ingredients do the talking."
